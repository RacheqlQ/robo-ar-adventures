🧑‍🏫 Grown-up & tutor guide

Learning goal: help children notice color, shape, order, quantity, and grouping. Ask the question first, wait for the child to answer, then invite them to explain the clue.

  • Missing colour: name a color and find an object with that color.
  • Pattern bug: say the sequence out loud and predict what repeats.
  • Shape safari: compare corners, curves, and sides.
  • Count with Robo: touch or point to each item once while counting.
  • Sort it out: group objects by one shared feature, then try a different rule.
  • Look & remember: show an order, hide it, and recall what came first/next.

Support: use two choices for a child who needs help. Celebrate effort, not speed. Stop if the child becomes tired.

Privacy: the camera feed is used for AR tracking in this browser; this page does not record or upload video intentionally. Use with adult supervision.
