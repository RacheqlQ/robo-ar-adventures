The Bot That Listens

Meet Echo, a little bot with big ears! Hear high and low sounds, try clear prompts, and see how noise makes listening hard.

Sound is on, so keep the volume low. This page does not use the microphone. The camera only shows the room behind the characters. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Grown-up guide

  1. Press Start AR and allow the camera. Turn the volume down low.
  2. Look ahead to find Echo floating in front of you. Tap Echo to say hello.
  3. High & low: tap each button, listen, and watch the sound waves.
  4. Prompts: compare a fuzzy prompt with a clear prompt.
  5. Noise: switch between a noisy room and a quiet room.
  6. Practice giving Echo a clear prompt out loud together.

Talk about it

  • What makes a prompt clear?
  • Why did Echo get confused in the noisy room?
  • What would you ask Echo to do?
