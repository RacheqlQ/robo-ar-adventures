Robo Learns to See

Step into Robo’s world! Find floating shapes, finish a pattern, and click a picture to see what a camera includes and leaves out.

Hold the device upright and move slowly to look around. The camera only shows the room behind the characters. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Grown-up guide

  1. Press Start AR and allow the camera. Hold the device upright.
  2. Turn slowly until you spot Robo’s floating shapes.
  3. Look: tap each shape and name its color and shape together.
  4. Pattern: tap the gray ball to finish red, blue, red, blue…
  5. Camera: tap to “click” a picture, then talk about what the frame included and left out.
  6. Remind your child that a computer’s guess is a guess to check.

Talk about it

  • What colors and shapes can you find in our room?
  • What did the camera leave out?
  • How can we check if Robo’s guess is right?

Class practice

  • Hunt for three red things, three round things, and one pattern in the classroom.
  • Build a block pattern, then ask a partner what comes next.
  • Hold up a paper frame to “take a photo,” then list what the frame left out.
