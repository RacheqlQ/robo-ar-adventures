The Sorting Basket

Help Robo sort floating toys into baskets! Follow a rule, catch a bug, fix it, and then change the rule.

Hold the device upright and move slowly to find the toys and baskets. The camera only shows the room behind the characters. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Grown-up guide

  1. Press Start AR and allow the camera. Hold the device upright.
  2. Look around to find the floating toys and the baskets below them.
  3. Sort by color: tap a toy, then tap the basket it belongs in.
  4. When a bug appears, name the mistake together and try again.
  5. Sort by shape: the rule changed! Sort again with the new rule.
  6. Steps: talk through notice, plan, sort, check, fix.

Talk about it

  • What is the first step of our plan?
  • How did we find and fix the mistake?
  • What other rule could we use to sort?
